Cinematic product shot. The camera is completely static: locked-off tripod, no zoom, no pan, no tilt, no angle change. A bright, airy primary school classroom in Cameroon with soft morning daylight, cream walls, a light wooden desk, a deep green mug and a deep green chalkboard in the background.
The black smartphone lying flat on the right side of the desk lifts off smoothly, glides through the air and turns to stand upright, then floats about 15 cm above the center of the handwritten lesson sheet, its screen facing the camera. As it settles, its screen lights up and shows the PEDAGO app with the lesson sheet, and a thin soft green light line sweeps across the paper beneath it, like a scan.
Smooth, slow, elegant, frictionless motion with gentle ease-in and ease-out, realistic physics, consistent soft shadows that follow the phone. The desk, the paper sheet, the mug, the pencil, the chalk box and the chalkboard stay perfectly still. Only one phone. No people, no hands, no morphing, no extra objects, no text overlay.
